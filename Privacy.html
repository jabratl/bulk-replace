Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Bulk Replace ("the app") is a free tool that lets you run find-and-replace across your own Google Docs. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app can:

  • List the folders and Google Docs in your Google Drive (read-only access), so you can choose which ones to process.
  • Read the text of the Google Docs you select, and apply the replacements you define, using the Google Docs API.

The app does not access any other Google data, and it does not read your email, contacts, calendar or photos.

How your data is handled

  • The app runs entirely in your web browser. Your document content, file names and replacement lists are processed on your device.
  • We do not operate a server that receives, stores, sells or shares your Google data. Your data is sent only between your browser and Google's own APIs.
  • Your Google access token is kept in your browser's memory during use and is not saved by us.
  • Settings such as your replacement lists, glossary, chosen folders and the OAuth Client ID you enter are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can clear them by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Your Google data is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by using the sign-out option inside the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
